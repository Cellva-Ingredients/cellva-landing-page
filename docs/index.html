Coffee Fruit Extract

Cocoa alternative from coffee

CoffeeCoa™

CoffeeCoa™ Dark is a coffee-derived powder that performs like cocoa in solid and baked applications. It brings a dark color, rich roasted notes and a cocoa-like sensory profile, giving manufacturers a Brazilian, coffee-based alternative for cocoa formulations.
